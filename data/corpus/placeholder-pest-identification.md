# Pest identification and management (PLACEHOLDER)

THIS DOCUMENT IS A PLACEHOLDER. It names no pest, states no threshold, recommends no
product, and gives no rate. It exists to exercise the pipeline.

## Why this file is empty of specifics

Pest management content is the highest-risk category in this corpus. A wrong
identification sends a farmer to the wrong intervention. A wrong product sends them to a
chemical that may be banned, ineffective, or harmful. A wrong rate can damage the crop,
the soil, or the person applying it. None of that is worth guessing at, so nothing is
guessed at here.

## Structure to be filled from a validated source

The real document will hold, per pest: the local names in English, Shona, and Ndebele,
the crop stages at risk, the visible symptoms a farmer can check in the field without
equipment, the distinguishing features against pests that look similar, the economic
threshold at which intervention is justified, non-chemical options first, and only then
registered chemical options with their exact label rates.

## Chemical control

No chemical control information is recorded in this placeholder. Product names, active
ingredients, application rates, mixing instructions, pre-harvest intervals, and re-entry
periods must come from the current registered product label and from Plant Protection
Research Institute guidance. Mhizha will not state any of these figures unless they are
present verbatim in a validated passage it can show the farmer.

## Correct behaviour today

If a farmer asks for a spray rate or a product name, Mhizha must refuse, explain that it
has no validated source for that, and refer them to the product label and their local
AGRITEX extension officer. The gap is registered as G-03 in data/SOURCES.md.
