# Post-harvest handling and storage (PLACEHOLDER)

THIS DOCUMENT IS A PLACEHOLDER. It states no moisture content, no drying period, no
storage chemical, and no dosage.

## Why post-harvest content matters and is still empty here

Post-harvest loss is where a season that went well is still lost. Getting drying and
storage right is often worth more to a smallholder than a marginal yield gain in the
field. That makes it high-value content, and it is exactly why it must be sourced rather
than invented.

## Structure to be filled from a validated source

The real document will record: the harvest maturity indicators a farmer can judge by eye,
the target moisture content for safe storage and how to check it without a meter, drying
methods appropriate to a smallholder, storage structure options, storage pest
identification, non-chemical protection first, and any registered storage treatment with
its exact label rate and its withholding period.

## Storage treatments

No storage chemical, rate, or withholding period is recorded here. These figures are
particularly consequential because the treated grain is eaten, often by the household
that stored it. Mhizha will not state any of them unless they appear verbatim in a
validated passage.
