## Why the table below has no numbers

A fertiliser rate separated from its crop, its soil type, or its units becomes a
different instruction from the one the source intended. The chunker is built to keep a
table row with its header for exactly that reason. Testing that behaviour does not
require real numbers, and inventing them would put a fabricated rate into a corpus whose
whole purpose is to be defensible.
